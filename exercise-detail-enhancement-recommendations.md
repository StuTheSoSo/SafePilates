# Pilates Exercise Detail Enhancement Recommendations

This document outlines advanced recommendations for further improving the exercise detail pages, with a focus on deepening user understanding, safety, and independent practice. Each section includes a detailed prompt for content authors or AI enrichment.

---

## 1. Add “Where to Feel It” or “Primary Sensation”
**Status:** ✅ Implemented for all exercises
**Why:** Helps users develop body awareness and self-correct form.

**Prompt:**
> For each exercise, add a short line describing the main area of muscular engagement or the "zone of effort." Use language like "Feel the work in..." or "Primary sensation: ...". Be specific (e.g., "lower abdominals and inner thighs"), and avoid generic phrases.

---

## 2. Include “Transition In/Out” Guidance
**Status:** ✅ Implemented for all exercises
**Why:** Ensures safe entry and exit, especially for apparatus or advanced mat work.

**Prompt:**
> Add a brief description of how to safely get into and out of the starting position for this exercise. Use clear, stepwise language. Example: "To exit, bend the knees and roll to your side before sitting up."

---

## 3. Add “Apparatus Settings” for Equipment-Based Exercises
**Status:** ✅ Implemented for all equipment-based exercises
**Why:** Provides clarity and consistency for Reformer, Chair, and Cadillac work.

**Prompt:**
> For each equipment-based exercise, specify recommended spring tension, footbar position, or strap length. Use a format like "Reformer: 1 medium spring, footbar in middle position." Include any safety checks for apparatus setup.

---

## 4. Highlight Contraindications or Special Populations
**Status:** ✅ Implemented for all exercises
**Why:** Increases safety for users with specific needs or risks.

**Prompt:**
> Add a line for contraindications or special populations. Use language like "Not recommended for..." or "Caution with osteoporosis, pregnancy, etc." Keep it concise and, if possible, use bold or icon cues for emphasis.

---

## 5. Suggest Repetition Ranges for Different Levels
**Status:** ✅ Implemented for all exercises
**Why:** Supports progression and individualized practice.

**Prompt:**
> Instead of a single rep count, provide a range for beginner/intermediate/advanced, or note when to focus on quality over quantity. Example: "Suggested reps: 5–8 (beginner), 8–12 (intermediate), 12+ (advanced)."

---

## 6. Add “Self-Check” or “Quick Correction” Tips
**Status:** ✅ Implemented for all exercises
**Why:** Encourages mindful, independent practice and reduces reliance on instructors.

**Prompt:**
> For each exercise, include a one-line self-check or quick correction cue. Example: "Check: Is your pelvis still level?" or "Quick tip: Keep the neck long and shoulders away from the ears."

---

## 7. Link to Video or Animated Demo (if available)
**Status:** ⏭️ Skipped (no videos available yet)
**Why:** Visual guidance accelerates learning and supports all learning styles.

**Prompt:**
> If a video or animation is available, add a link or icon to the exercise detail. If not, include a placeholder or note for future content. Example: "[Watch demo video]" or a video icon.
