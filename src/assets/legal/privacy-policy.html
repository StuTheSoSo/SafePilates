PilateSafe Privacy Policy

Effective date: April 11, 2026

Introduction

This Privacy Policy applies to PilateSafe apps distributed through the Apple App Store and Google Play Store.

Developer: PilateSafe. Support email: support@pilatesafe.app.

Data collection

PilateSafe does not collect personal information from users during normal app operation.

The app stores only local device data such as selected theme preferences and an on-device daily AI usage counter used to limit AI fallback requests.

The app does not collect or transmit health data, contact data, location data, camera or microphone input, or device identifiers for analytics.

Local storage and bundled assets

PilateSafe is designed for offline-first use. Exercise guidance, contraindications, and program content are bundled locally within the app.

If on-device AI is enabled, model assets are loaded from local app files and not requested from a remote service.

Network behavior

PilateSafe does not transmit your selected conditions, entered text, or personal data to external servers during normal use.

Network calls are limited to loading bundled app assets and any future app updates obtained through the app distribution channel.

Third-party sharing

The app does not share user data with analytics, advertising, or tracking providers.

No personal data is sold or disclosed to third parties.

No third-party SDKs are used for data collection by default.

Children’s privacy

This app is not directed to children under the age of 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Data retention and deletion

Local preferences and counters are retained on the device until the app is uninstalled or the app storage is cleared.

To delete local data, uninstall the app or clear the app storage in your device settings.

Security

The app uses standard Ionic/Capacitor WebView storage and local browser storage. It does not request permissions for contacts, location, camera, microphone, or other sensitive device features as part of core functionality.

Policy updates

This policy may be revised to reflect app updates or legal requirements. The effective date above will change when updates occur.

Contact

If you have questions about this policy, email support@pilatesafe.app or use the support information included in the app store listing.
